# PR 43 — the epoch ring (foundation): as-landed design record

Status: landed 2026-07-09 on `epoch-integration` (4 sub-commits). Contract: `doc/sim-draw-pr43-44-contract-refresh.md` §1 (channel list), §2 (the PR-43 spec), §7 (BINDING rulings, esp. §7.1 flat-copies / §7.3 query keying / §7.7 shell-sourced DEAD_THIS_BATCH). This file records the as-landed shape, the enumerated lockstep exceptions, and the 44a/44b handoff points.

## What landed, per §2 item

1. **The ring (§2.1).** `SimSnapshot` replaced its front/back double buffer with `EPOCH_RING_SLOTS = 3` slots, each holding all six row namespaces + `EpochSlotMeta {u64 epochId; frame span [firstSimFrame, lastSimFrame]; atomic refCount; per-slot channel-version scalars (cmdQueueCacheEpoch, pieceCacheEpoch, mirrorDrainSerial)}`. `Update()` is the producer half: extract everything into a free slot (`PickFreeSlot`: refcount 0, neither newest nor held), publish it as newest-complete, `epochId = ++epochCounter`. `AcquireNewestEpoch()` is the consumer half, called at barrier step 3a: ref++ newest, ref-- previously held; a refcount hitting 0 RETIRES that epoch and the barrier runs the retirement hooks. All copies are FLAT per §7.1 (no refcount-shared blocks). Under 43's lockstep this is a pointer rotation with the double buffer's values; N=3 is exercised, not yet load-bearing.
   - **Enumerated lockstep exception (dissolves at 44a):** a boundary whose unit-row due-check does not fire still refreshes the net-mutable channels (team/player/global rows) IN PLACE into the held slot — value-identical to the pre-ring behavior (they re-extracted + swapped unconditionally every boundary, independent of the unit rows). Under the producer flip every epoch is fully extracted at the sim frame edge and this in-place refresh goes away.
   - **Per-slot channel scalars:** the physical cmd-queue/piece caches and mirror layers remain singletons whose content tracks the newest epoch (lockstep); the slot meta records their versions at publish (`SealEpochChannelVersions`, barrier step 3b''). Physically per-slot channel COPIES arrive with 44a where N-buffering becomes load-bearing.
   - Frame span note: `firstSimFrame > lastSimFrame` denotes a same-frame republish forced by a between-frames net mutation (`MarkMutatedOutsideFrame`).

2. **Deleter release rekeyed to epoch retirement (§2.2).** Split path: barrier step 8 acks via `AckDrainedDestroysEpoch(EpochId())` — destruct+poison, TAG with the epoch whose record dispatch just completed, NO auto-release; `ReleaseRetired(retiredEpochId)` (barrier step 3a, on retirement) returns every poisoned shell with `ackEpoch <= retired` to its pool. Replaces the end-of-Draw `ReleaseAcked()` (now flag-off-only) and the "next ack releases leftovers" special case. The valve service keeps ack+release-all in place — the documented lockstep-degenerate "epoch retires in place" (it must free pages mid-frame; poisoned shells are never legally read, so early pool return is address-blind). Under lockstep ack (barrier N step 8) and release (barrier N+1 step 3a) coincide per epoch, which is what keeps 43 flag-on-identical.

3. **DEAD_THIS_BATCH, amended per §7.7 (producer shell-sourced).** Tri-state `SimSnapshotValid {INACTIVE, ACTIVE, DEAD_THIS_BATCH}`; `Valid()` serves DEAD_THIS_BATCH only while the boundary dispatch window is open (`BoundaryShellWindowActive`). The PRODUCER (`SimSnapshot::ExtractDeadRowsFromShells`, inside `Update()` after the live extraction) extracts the batch's dying ids' rows directly from their DeferredObjectDeleter shells, sourced from `RenderEventQueue::BoundaryDeadUnits/Features/Projectiles` (id→shell maps; the projectile map is populated for SYNCED destroy records only — the `fd41dbdd92` namespace filter, kept). Genuine at-death state by construction ⇒ the 38b genuineness-guard class (`bbdab4a5f4`) is DELETED, not guarded. INACTIVE-guarded so same-batch id reuse stays ACTIVE. Grow-only feature/projectile rows are pre-sized to cover died-in-batch ids (`minSlots`).
   - **Shell-readable surface:** plain fields are genuine; sub-blocks PreDestruct destroyed read DOCUMENTED DEFAULTS — unit commandAI/moveType/weapons (weapon family = count-0 nil shape, moveTypeKind unknown, build-state none, estPath none, repairBelowHealth -1, repeatOrders 0), dependence-severed pointers (lastAttacker/transporter/transportees = none), projectile damage array (nil shape) and weapon-projectile target OBJECT (degrades to its stored ground pos, 'g' form). Feature rows are fully genuine (no destroyed sub-blocks in their surface). Map-backed per-allyteam answers (InRadar/InLos/...) are computed with shell inputs against the live post-death maps. **KEEP-IN-SYNC duty:** a field added to Extract/ExtractFeatures/ExtractProjectiles must be mirrored or explicitly defaulted in `ExtractDeadRowsFromShells`, or DEAD rows serve stale slot garbage for it.
   - **Inert-but-armed under 43:** the barrier dispatches still read LIVE under `barrierLive` (43 does not convert them — that is 44b); the id-coverage gate is the rows' first consumer. Enumeration/spatial twins, the pick grid and the diff gate admit ACTIVE rows only (the 38b guards, reapplied). Marks are cleared at the step-8 window close (`ClearDeadThisBatch`); slot recycle would also kill them.
   - The 44b consumer note: the defaulted deep sub-blocks are exactly the reads §3.6b's event-time capture must cover (fire-time value args), not row reads — carried into the 44b design.

3b. **`IdToObject` shell-fallback retirement (deferred from SCOPE-1).** The drawer render records now RETAIN a died-in-batch id past its destroy-record dispatch: `RenderUnitDestroyed`/`RenderFeatureDestroyed` keep the record with `obj` = the shell (readable until the step-8 ack) and push (id, shell) onto `deadRetainedRecords`; `ClearDeadRetainedRecords()` (barrier step 8 + valve service, i.e. retention extends exactly TO THE ACK) clears them with an `obj == shell` guard against same-batch id reuse. The feature destroy dispatch (re)assigns `obj/def` unconditionally so a NON-model feature created-and-died in the same batch — the id class `FindPendingDestroyFeature` cannot cover and the `unit_healthbars_widget_forwarding` nil-storm came from — gets an at-death record too. On this retention, `LuaUtils::IdToObject<CUnit/CFeature>` resolves purely via `DrawerGetObjectByID` (no shell fallback, both branches); `LuaSnapshotServe::ResolveDrawUnit/Feature` and the drawflag live paths dropped their fallback legs likewise. **Survivors (deliberate):** `LuaSyncedRead` `ParseRawUnit`/`ParseFeature` keep `SimDrawSplit::ShellFallbackUnit/Feature` — they are the live-parse leg of the `barrierLive` dispatches 43 does not convert; 44b replaces them with the DEAD_THIS_BATCH twins (§3.6). `SimDrawSplit::ShellFallback*` + the `RenderEventQueue` dead-shell maps therefore stay (the maps now also feed the §7.7 producer extraction).

4. **Query/reply keying (§7.3, Batch-4 ruling).** `TestBuildOrder`: reply key = the build-grid-SNAPPED pos, canonicalized at query build (replicates `Pos2BuildPos`' x/z arithmetic over immutable def footprint + facing; y zeroed — `GetBuildHeight` ignores input y). Idempotent, so evaluating the snapped query equals evaluating the raw one (the armed dual-run bit-verifies). `ClosestBuildPos`/`TestMoveOrder` + the pos-form weapon traces (TryTarget/TestTarget/TestRange pos form, HFLOF pos variants): STANDING-LATEST-QUERY — replies keyed pos-agnostically (float payload zeroed), the barrier dedupes pending by standing key keeping the most recently registered position, evaluates that, and the serve is a standing-key lookup: ≤1 boundary late, never-default. Enumerated deviations: ≤1-boundary-old cursor position; same-frame standing-key collisions resolve last-registered-wins (ruled acceptable).

5. **Cache re-keying (§2.5).** `SimSnapshot::EpochId()` (u64, monotonic per game, teardown-reset) replaced `Generation()` (u32) everywhere: cmd-queue cache, piece cache, team-unit index, quad-membership mirrors (unit/feature + projectile), `SnapshotPickGrid`, the pregame `== 0` fallbacks and the event-override `!= 0` guards. The ClearCaches cross-game-aliasing rationale dissolves (u64 monotonic + explicit slots).

6. **The armed id-coverage gate (§2.6/§3.6a).** `RenderEventQueue` collects every record-referenced id during the drain (units, features, SYNCED-namespace projectiles; unsynced projectile records excluded by design — no row namespace); after publish+acquire the barrier runs `SimSnapshot::CheckEpochIdCoverage()`: each id must be ACTIVE or DEAD_THIS_BATCH in the published epoch. Violations are deterministic `[EpochIdCoverage]` L_ERROR lines (capped at 50) + counters reported by `/epochstats` and at teardown — the 38b failure class becomes a greppable gate failure. Always armed under the split (cost: a map-free array walk over the batch's record ids). **Enumerated limitations:** the `UnsyncedBoundaryQueue` a0 closures are type-erased and not introspectable — their id coverage is the 44 §3.8 drain-time re-resolution audit's duty, not this gate's; the valve service cannot check (it does not publish) and drops its refs unchecked.

7. **Piece/SSBO channel representation (§4.7/§7.6) — settled for 43.** The piece-transform channel is represented as: (a) `RefreshPieces`' flat per-object capture, produced at the barrier and re-keyed to `EpochId` (its version recorded per-slot at seal time), and (b) the PR-8 `ExtractTransforms`/`transformsMemStorage` SSBO staging, which under 43 still runs in the UPD pass INSIDE the extended park window (i.e. produced under the park, camera-independent per the PR-8 contract). Per §7.6 (ruling A) the physical move of `transformsMemStorage` production to the sim thread with per-epoch staging is 44a's work at the uploader seam — no double copy was introduced here. S0 churn re-measure: non-gating, folded into the §7.1 standing TODO via the `/epochstats` channel-bytes telemetry (also auto-logged at teardown of every split gate run).

8. **`/epochstats` telemetry (§2.8).** New unsynced action: ring occupancy (per-slot epochId/frame span/refcount/held/newest + sealed channel versions), per-channel payload bytes of the held epoch (unit flat, weapon flat, unit/feature/team rules-params entry-walks, projectile/feature flat, team stats history) and the id-coverage counters. Also auto-logged at `SimSnapshot::Clear()` (teardown) when the split ran, so every gate run leaves measured §7.1 numbers.

## Invariant notes (§2.6)

- Flag-off bit-identity: the ring is a pure representation swap; every DEAD_THIS_BATCH/retention/coverage/deleter-rekey path is `SimDrawSplit`-gated; the retired shell-fallback legs were provably inert flag-off (the shell window only opens under the split). Proven by the armed diff-gate resim (0 mismatches, 0 DESYNC).
- Flag-on value/timing-identity: records are never skipped and dispatch in unchanged total fire order (the drain is untouched); the epoch is a container swap. The only timing change is the pool-RETURN point of poisoned shells (end-of-Draw → epoch retirement), which is address-blind and unobservable.
- Records-fold rule (§1.3): under lockstep every barrier consumes its batch (today's catch-up batching IS the fold); the producer-side fold-into-next-epoch becomes real at 44a.

## 44a/44b handoff points

- 44a: per-slot physical copies for the cmd/piece/mirror channels; `transformsMemStorage` per-epoch staging at the uploader seam (§7.6 A); the producer moves to `SimThreadProc`'s frame edge and the in-place team/player/glob refresh + the barrier-time `ExtractDeadRowsFromShells` fold into it; `CacheMapStartPositions` moves to load.
- 44b: convert the `barrierLive` dispatches to read published state — the surviving `ParseRawUnit`/`ParseFeature` shell fallbacks retire onto the DEAD_THIS_BATCH twins; the id-coverage gate (landed, armed) is the deterministic detector for that conversion; dispatch-window scoping becomes per-epoch (§3.6c) — today's single `BoundaryShellWindow` bool is the lockstep-degenerate form of "epoch E's records are dispatching".
