# PR 44a — extraction on the sim thread (residual consume-park): as-landed design record

Status: landed 2026-07-09/10 on `epoch-integration`. Contract: `doc/sim-draw-pr43-44-contract-refresh.md` §3.1/§3.2/§3.7 (producer/pacing/culling) + §7.5 (the 44a/b/c split) + §7.6A (transformsMemStorage staging) + §8 (Gap-B paused-servicing note); builds directly on the PR-43 design record (`doc/sim-draw-pr43-epoch-ring-design.md`) and dissolves its enumerated lockstep deviations. This file records the as-landed shape, the enumerated deviations/residuals, and the 44b handoff points.

## The shape

**Producer** (`CGame::ProduceEpochAtSimEdge`, called at the `SimThreadProc` loop top — a sim frame edge; the inter-packet yield point does NOT produce): with the split running, epoch production happens on the SIM thread, overlapped with the draw thread's rendering. Order per §3.1:

1. **Ctrl-poke drain** — `LuaSplitContract::DrainBoundaryApplies()` moved from barrier step 2b to the producer, immediately before the due-check (freshness by construction, §1.8). The queue gained a mutex (draw-context Lua enqueues concurrently with the sim-thread drain). An applied poke marks `MutatedOutsideFrame` → forces a republish.
2. **Pacing** (§3.2 skip-if-unconsumed) — `SimSnapshot::NewestEpochConsumed()`: the consumer stamps `consumedEpochId` (release) inside `AcquireNewestEpoch`; the producer publishes only when the newest epoch is consumed. Extraction cost is paid at min(sim rate, draw rate); a catch-up burst pays ~one extraction per draw frame, and the sealed record batch covers exactly one burst (steady one-draw-frame lag, no backlog growth).
3. **Due-check** — rows due (`ProduceDue()`: new frames / aliveCount change / mutation mark), OR pending records/deferred closures (they must ride an epoch to ever dispatch — e.g. net-driven chat pokes while paused), OR a 250 ms fallback-republish timer (covers between-frames mutation classes with no mark, e.g. the main-thread `clientPaused` flip; also refreshes team/player/global rows at ≥4 Hz while paused, replacing the deleted in-place refresh's per-draw-frame freshness — enumerated deviation below).
4. **Rows** — `SimSnapshot::BeginEpochProduction()` → the shared produce body extracts all six namespaces + §7.7 DEAD_THIS_BATCH rows into a free ring slot and stages its meta (epochId = counter+1) without publishing.
5. **Per-slot channels** — `LuaSnapshotServe::RefreshCommandQueues(slot, epoch)`, `RefreshPieces(slot, epoch)`, `drawMapMirrors.DrainAtBarrier(slot)`.
6. **Transforms** (§7.6 ruling A) — `CUnitDrawer/CFeatureDrawer::ExtractTransformsAtSimEdge()`: the PR-8 `ExtractTransforms` (camera-independent) runs on the sim thread, FORCED-SERIAL (no fork-join of the shared thread pool concurrently with main-thread draw work), writing the singleton `transformsMemStorage` + its update list. No double copy: the storage IS the per-epoch staging; the SSBO upload consumes it once per epoch at the consumer's park (below).
7. **Queries** (§7.3) — `LuaSnapshotServe::EvaluateQueriesAtSimEdge()`: steals the (now mutex-guarded) pending trace+placement queues, evaluates the exact live predicates at the SAME edge the rows were extracted (reply consistent with the epoch's rows), MERGES results into staged reply maps. Also serviced when production is skipped and while the game is paused (nothing changed since the newest epoch → trivially consistent) — the §8 Gap-B servicing pattern, so cursor-tracking widget predicates stay live while paused.
8. **Seals** — `renderEventQueue.SealEpochBatch()` + `UnsyncedBoundaryQueue::SealEpochBatch()` + `deferredObjectDeleter.SealPendingBatch()`: the pending records / a0 closures / deferred shells become the epoch's batch. No sim frame can run between extraction and seal (same thread), so seal scope == extraction scope.
9. **Publish** — `SimSnapshot::PublishEpoch(slot, cmdEpoch, pieceEpoch, mirrorSerial)`: seals the channel versions into the slot meta, bumps the counter, release-stores `newestSlot`.

**Consumer** (`CGame::SimDrawBarrier`, flip branch; the park is RESIDUAL but real — §7.5): AcquireSimPause parks the sim at a frame edge exactly as before, then:

- step 1 = `renderEventQueue.DispatchSealedBatch()` — EXACTLY the epoch's sealed records, in fire order, still under `barrierLive` reading the parked live sim (44a does NOT convert the deferred serving — that is 44b). The post-seal tail (records fired after the epoch's edge) folds into the next epoch (§1.3).
- `DeliverBoundaryDeaths` + `SnapshotEffectContainers` unchanged (park-time live reads).
- step 3a = `AcquireNewestEpoch` (+ `ReleaseRetired`) + `drawMapMirrors.SetServingSlot(held)`.
- step 3a' = the armed id-coverage gate, unchanged (refs collected during the sealed dispatch check against the acquired epoch — holds by construction: a record id was either alive at the epoch's edge (ACTIVE) or died in its batch (DEAD_THIS_BATCH, ledger-sourced)).
- flip replaces steps 2c/3/3b/3b'/3b''/3c/3d with `CommitStagedQueryReplies()` (swap the staged reply maps into the served ones under the park).
- step 3e `EvaluateDefaultCmdQuery` STAYS a consumer-side park-time hook — **enumerated 44a residual**: it is main-thread-bound (fires the `DefaultCommand` widget callin, walks UI receivers, reads camera/mouse, and reaches `CSyncedLuaHandle::AllowUnitTransport`); the prerequisites doc already assigns its sim-thread servicing design to 44b.
- steps 5/6 (movers, heightmap rect drain) unchanged.
- step 7 = `UnsyncedBoundaryQueue::DrainSealedBatch()` — the epoch's sealed closures only.
- step 8 = window close + `AckSealedDestroysEpoch(HeldEpochId())` — acks ONLY the sealed shell prefix; deaths after the epoch's edge have undispatched destroy records (the next epoch's batch) and stay readable.
- In `UpdateUnsynced`, `worldDrawer.Update` still runs under the park (SCOPE-1 record refresh / drawPos / uniforms / culling read live-parked state — unchanged); under the flip `transformsUploader.Update()` moved INSIDE the park right after it (the storage is producer-written; a post-release upload would race the producer). `modelUniformsUploader` stays post-release (draw-written storage).

The **lockstep path** (flag-off, and flag-on pre-spawn / sim-thread-dead) keeps produce-at-barrier verbatim, adapted to the per-slot channels: mirror drain + cache refreshes now run right AFTER `Update()`+acquire into the held slot (value-identical by park quiescence; the diff-gate memcmp still follows them).

## PR-43 lockstep deviations dissolved

1. **Cmd-queue / piece caches → true per-slot copies.** `cmdQueueCaches[EPOCH_RING_SLOTS]` / `unitPieceCaches` / `featurePieceCaches` + per-slot epoch stamps. The producer refreshes the produced slot; every serving read goes through the consumer-held slot (`ServedCmdCache()` / the piece-slot getters). The version-gated per-unit recopy compares against THAT SLOT's previous content (≤ ring-depth boundaries old) — correct by construction, ~ring-depth × the sparse per-boundary copy volume. The model-meta cache (shared immutable) became pointer-stable (`unique_ptr` values) + mutex (producer inserts vs draw-side lookups).
2. **Mirror layers → true per-slot copies.** `DrawMapMirrors` payloads went per-ring-slot; whole-layer dirtiness became monotonic per-layer VERSIONS (choke points bump; each slot records what it last copied — a slot re-copies exactly the layers that moved since IT was produced). Queries read the serving slot (set at acquire); the producer fills the produced slot. Applies flag-off too (the ring already rotates flag-off since PR 43) — near-static layers stay version-gated no-ops, churning layers (LOS/extraction/blocking) copied per-drain exactly as before.
3. **No-publish in-place team/player/global refresh → flip-dead.** The flip producer re-extracts a full epoch on the mutation MARKS added to the mutating `ClientReadNet` handlers (NETMSG_PLAYERINFO/PAUSE/INTERNAL_SPEED/USER_SPEED/PLAYERLEFT/SETSHARE/TEAM; `CUnit::ChangedTeam` already marked) + the 250 ms fallback. The in-place refresh remains ONLY as the lockstep (flag-off/pre-spawn) representation, where it is required for flag-off byte-identity.
4. **The valve** keeps the in-place flush/ack/release-all (it must free pages mid-frame; the "sim waits for epoch retirement" rekey + in-place-drain deletion is explicitly 44c §3.4, per the 43/44 handoff list) but is now **sealed-batch-aware**: `Flush()`/`Drain()`/`AckDrainedDestroys()` consume any sealed-unconsumed batch along with the tail and reset the seal marks, so the later barrier acquire of that epoch cannot double-dispatch or double-ack. Valve coverage refs stay dropped unchecked (43's documented limitation).

## New cross-thread machinery (and what stays lock-free)

- Real mutexes: the ctrl-poke queue (`LuaSplitContract`), the trace/placement pending queues + staged reply maps (`queryChannelMtx`), the model-meta cache. All uncontended in practice.
- `SimSnapshot`: `epochCounter`/`consumedEpochId`/`heldSlot`/`newestSlot` became atomics; publish = release store on `newestSlot`, acquire = acquire load; the consumed signal is stored AFTER the ref++/held update (release) so the producer's slot recycling orders against it. Everything else crossing threads is fenced by the park handshake (the sim's pre-park writes happen-before the main thread's post-`RequestPause` reads; the consumer's park-time writes happen-before the sim's post-release reads).
- **`HeldEpochId()` re-key**: `EpochId()` now advances mid-draw-frame (the producer publishes concurrently), so every draw-side derived cache (team-unit index, quad-membership mirrors, pick grid, override guards, pregame `== 0` fallbacks, the step-8 ack key) re-keyed to the held slot's epoch id — main-thread stable between acquires. A missed conversion would be a stale-cache/torn-u64 bug class; grep for `simSnapshot.EpochId()` when touching draw-side code.
- **Dead-row source union**: at a produce edge the dispatch-time dead maps are empty (the records have not dispatched); the producer sources §7.7 dead rows from the `pendingDestroyShells` ledger instead (`CollectPendingDeadShells`, newest generation per id, `fd41dbdd92` synced-projectile filter). Under lockstep the ledger is empty and the maps are the source — the union covers both, and produce-only-after-consume guarantees the ledger holds exactly the batch's deaths.
- **`ShellFallbackUnit/Feature` extension**: an id referenced by epoch E's records can die AFTER E's edge, before its destroy record dispatches (it rides E+1); the dispatch-time dead maps do not know it, so the fallback now also consults the pending-destroy ledger (readable: its shell is post-seal, un-acked). Lockstep leaves the ledger empty inside the window — no behavior change there.
- **New-object pose catch-up**: the producer only covers objects registered as of the previous consume; objects created by a consume's record dispatch get a targeted `ExtractPendingNewObjectTransforms()` (ids queued in `AddObject` under the flip) inside the drawer `Update()` under the park, so their first rendered frame has a valid pose. Created-and-died-in-batch ids are skipped (the transform alloc is already gone). GATE-FOUND FIX: the catch-up resolves ids via the drawer RENDER RECORD ONLY (live pointer | readable retained shell | nullptr — records are cleared before their shells poison) and skips null; the first strict ATG run SIGSEGV'd (f=11826) because `DrawerGetObjectByID`'s live-resolve fallback can return nullptr (NDEBUG-dead assert) for an id whose lifetime ended between the push and the call.
- `CacheMapStartPositions` pre-fills at `SpawnSimThread` (main thread; the MapParser must stay off the sim thread); asserted in `BeginEpochProduction`.

## Enumerated deviations / accepted residuals (flag-on only)

- **Rows lag the park point.** The held epoch was extracted at the first frame edge after the previous consume; the sim may run ahead before the park (≤1 sim frame at 1× by backpressure; up to one burst under FF free-run). Consequences: (a) served rows/records are ≤1 consume-interval older than under 43 — the §3.3 staleness contract; (b) record/closure dispatch lags one extra draw frame vs 43 under catch-up (the §1.3 fold rule); (c) the park-time live reads (drawPos, record scalars, uniforms, heightmap rects, `EvaluateDefaultCmdQuery`) are at the park frame F_P while rows/transforms are at the epoch edge F_E — a model-pose vs healthbar/icon offset of ≤1 sim frame at 1× (invisible under interpolation), up to a burst under FF. 44b/44c move the remaining park-time reads onto the epoch.
- **Paused-game team/player/global freshness** drops from per-draw-frame (43's in-place refresh) to mark-driven + ≥4 Hz (fallback republish) — ping/cpu display class only.
- **Draw-side derived caches rebuild at ≤4 Hz while paused** (fallback republishes bump the held epoch id).
- **Armed diff-gate / armed dual-run flag-ON is stale-vs-live by design now** (held rows F_E vs live F_P): the armed comparisons are only meaningful flag-OFF under the flip (the gate policy already runs them flag-OFF). The strict (`SplitDrawContract=2`) and id-coverage gates are unaffected.
- **Known benign torn reads added to the §C family**: producer-side reads of `gu->spectatingFullView/myAllyTeam`, `game->IsGameOver/IsClientPaused/IsDoneLoading/IsSavedGame` (main-thread bools), and — with `SplitDrawContract=0` — direct draw-thread unsynced ctrl writes (e.g. `SetTeamColor` without the poke queue) racing the producer's extraction reads (contract-on routes them through the poke queue). BoundaryStats counters are relaxed atomics (already).
- The **`Misc::SimDrawBarrier`/`Misc::SimPauseWait` profiler timers** keep their names; under the flip the barrier timer covers the consume-only body.

## Telemetry (measured, gate runs 2026-07-09/10)

`[SimParkStats] parks=N totalMs=X avgMs=Y maxMs=Z` at teardown (with the `[SimPauseSurvey]` dump) — the parked-window aggregate, the program's headline number. Plus the PR-43 `[EpochStats]` teardown lines (now: cmd/piece bytes are the held slot's per-slot copy).

- **Parked window (flag-ON, FF full-length):** strict ATG parks=34742 avg **2.46 ms** max 299.8 ms; headful Rosetta (final binary) parks=24999 avg **2.14 ms** max 164 ms — vs the PR-42-measured pre-flip full-barrier park of **~13.5 ms** under FF catch-up (~5.5–6.3× shrink; the residual is dispatch + movers + heightmap + the `worldDrawer.Update` record-refresh/culling half + the in-park SSBO upload).
- **Frame-idle republish gate (headful-gate-found fix):** the first headful Rosetta run showed 13 instancevbotable ZOMBIE-recovery echoes (unitlights/healthbars/stencils; a class PR-43 runs never showed on Rosetta) — root-caused to mid-play SAME-FRAME republishes (mutation-mark/fallback classes) dispatching widget events without a perceived GetGameFrame advance. After gating those classes on ≥100 ms frame-idleness: **0 zombies** over the full replay, and extraction count dropped 23,710 → 18,855 (the ~4.9k removed extractions were exactly those republishes).
- **FF wall-time note (operator attention):** full-length FF runs take longer than under PR-43 (strict ATG 12:35 vs 6:57; headful Rosetta 5:58 vs 4:05). Quantified: the extraction COUNT roughly tracks the now-unburdened (≈2× faster) draw loop's consume rate — the §3.2 pacing produces once per consume, and per-extraction cost is flat (1.85–3.46 ms avg vs 3.26 pre-flip) — so extraction cost is paid at DRAW rate on the SIM thread, which is the FF bottleneck. This is the contract's anticipated shape ("a catch-up burst pays ~one extraction per draw frame"), but FF throughput regresses; candidate knobs for an operator ruling (not landed): a producer min-publish-interval under free-run/catch-up, and/or re-enabling MT extraction on the sim thread with a fork-join fence against draw-side pool use.

## 44b handoff points (carried forward)

- Convert the `barrierLive` sealed dispatches to read PUBLISHED epoch state (the §3.6 creation/event-time serving); the id-coverage gate (armed, green under 44a) is the deterministic detector.
- `EvaluateDefaultCmdQuery` must move to the sim thread (split its sim-read half from the UI/widget-callin half); with no park, the paused-idle servicing added here for trace/placement generalizes to it.
- The staged-reply commit and the `SnapshotEffectContainers`/movers/heightmap/`worldDrawer.Update` park-time reads need no-park forms.
- Dispatch-window scoping per epoch (§3.6c): today's single `BoundaryShellWindow` bool is still the lockstep-degenerate form.
- The transforms uploader consumes the singleton storage under the park; with no park (44b) it needs a handoff buffer or per-slot staging at the uploader seam.
